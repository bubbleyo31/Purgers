Game / Map(Z:-263) rendering diagnosis — 2026-10-20

Conclusion
The stationary camera reversal symptom was reproduced at all four active
MapChunk_Prototype entrance spawn points. Rendering workload, especially the
number of object/pass submissions, is the principal cause of the measured
difference. SSAO's depth/normal prepass substantially amplifies that workload.
Main-light shadows contribute, but removing shadows alone does not eliminate it.
Physics simulation is only approximately 0.03–0.08 ms/frame in this idle test.

CPU versus GPU
The evidence prioritizes CPU/render submission and geometry/pass count over
pixel shading/fill-rate:
- Main-thread URP render CPU duration rises from approximately 1.7 to 11.7 ms.
- ScriptableRenderContext.Submit alone is 9.47 ms in the confirmed city baseline,
  falling to 6.05 ms without SSAO. These scopes include submission/synchronization;
  they are elapsed durations, not measurements of CPU utilization or exclusive work.
- Halving both render-target dimensions (quarter pixel count) gives essentially
  no improvement in the first controlled comparison: render CPU 11.94 -> 11.80 ms,
  camera GPU median 11.09 -> 10.70 ms, draw calls unchanged around 18,159.
- GPU elapsed time also rises with the city view. GPU timing can include bubbles
  while waiting for work. It should not be added to CPU duration.
- NVIDIA whole-device samples during the SSAO comparison showed 6–40% GPU use and
  variable 255–1710 MHz graphics clocks. These samples are supporting context,
  not a Unity-exclusive utilization measurement or controlled clock benchmark.
The data establish a submission/pass-count bottleneck; they do not prove that
all cost is CPU-only or exclude GPU geometry work.

Limitations
This is a controlled background Editor rendering comparison, not a Development
Player capture of actual multiplayer gameplay. Full Editor frame times cannot
be translated into promised in-game FPS. It omits actual player/HUD/postprocess
and combat workload, and GPU power management varied. No production optimization,
collision streaming, geometry merge, bake, scene save or prefab save was made.

Next optimization priorities to review, not implemented
1. Reduce the number of objects/passes submitted in the city view. Evaluate the
   SSAO depth/normal requirement and acceptable alternative settings first.
2. Evaluate visibility/occlusion and spatial grouping of building renderers;
   any grouping must preserve useful culling granularity. The current map has
   no LODGroup coverage or occluder/occludee static flags.
3. Evaluate shadow distance/cascade/object participation against visual needs.
4. Re-measure the chosen change in a Development Player and actual gameplay.
Collider activation is not the first intervention suggested by this evidence.
